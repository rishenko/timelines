# Data licence

The app's code is MIT (see `LICENSE`). The timeline data is licensed separately:

- **Timelines and their sources** (`data-src/`, `public/timelines/`): [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
  International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Reuse, adapt and share them, with
  credit to this project and to the sources each timeline cites.
- **Event maps** (`public/maps/`): CC BY 4.0, built from [Cliopatria](https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria)
  (Seshat Global History Databank, CC BY 4.0) and [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) (public
  domain). Credit Cliopatria as well.

**Exceptions.** Third-party material keeps its own licence:

- Quoted Wikipedia text stays under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), attributed
  through the linked article. That is the sentences quoted in source locators, and descriptions that closely follow a
  cited sentence. Everything else in the data (dates, rows, links, categories and descriptions written for this
  project) is CC BY 4.0.
- Images shown from Wikimedia Commons keep the licence given with each one.
- Data taken in bulk from a source under another licence is published only in its own file, under that
  source's licence.

The licence covers this project's own compilation and wording only. It grants no rights in names, trademarks or
other material that belongs to third parties.
